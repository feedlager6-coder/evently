# D4.2 Organizer Insights — Unified Organizer Analytics & Insights

## 1. Overview & Product Goal

Sprint **D4.2** transforms raw operational analytics into a cohesive, high-signal **Organizer Insights** system. An organizer opening Ivently can understand in under 10 seconds:
1. How their events are performing across views, interest, and confirmed RSVPs.
2. How their audience is growing across their organizations (total followers, 7-day velocity, 30-day velocity, unique engaged reach).
3. The exact impact and attribution of Telegram broadcast campaigns.
4. How viewers discover events via a verified source breakdown.
5. A single, 100% grounded factual summary sentence.

---

## 2. The Three Performance Pillars

### Pillar 1: Audience & Growth (Аудитория и рост)
* **Total Subscribers (`total_subscribers`)**: Aggregate follower count across all organizations owned by the organizer.
* **7-Day Velocity (`new_subscribers_7d`)**: Verified new subscriptions registered in the last 7 calendar days (`created_at >= now() - 7 days`).
* **30-Day Velocity (`new_subscribers_30d`)**: Verified new subscriptions registered in the last 30 calendar days (`created_at >= now() - 30 days`).
* **Unique Engaged Reach (`total_unique_engaged`)**: Deduplicated union count of unique users who have subscribed to any owned organization, expressed interest in any organizer event, or confirmed attendance (RSVP) to any organizer event.

### Pillar 2: Event Performance Totals (События)
* **Total Events (`total_events`)**: Total count of events submitted by the organizer.
* **Active / Upcoming Events (`upcoming_events_count`)**: Events with `start_at >= now()` that are published or pending review (excluding cancelled).
* **Past Events (`past_events_count`)**: Completed or archived events (`start_at < now()`).
* **Total Views (`total_views`)**: Total verified event views from `EventView`, deduplicated by the platform's 2-hour window rule.
* **Total Interest (`total_interest`)**: Total users who clicked "Хочу пойти" (`EventInterest`).
* **Total Confirmed RSVPs (`total_rsvps`)**: Total users who confirmed attendance "Я иду" (`EventAttendee`).

### Pillar 3: Telegram Broadcast Attribution (Рассылки в Telegram)
* **Total Campaigns (`total_broadcasts`)**: Number of broadcast campaigns sent by the organizer.
* **Total Delivered (`total_delivered`)**: Sum of messages successfully delivered to Telegram recipients.
* **Total Opened (`total_opened`)**: Recipient Mini App opens attributable to broadcast deep-links (`BroadcastRecipient.opened_at is not null`).
* **Attributed Interest (`total_attributed_interest`)**: Users who expressed interest after receiving and opening the broadcast (`attributed_interest_at is not null`).
* **Attributed RSVPs (`total_attributed_rsvp`)**: Users who confirmed attendance after opening the broadcast (`attributed_rsvp_at is not null`).
* **Overall Open Rate (`overall_open_rate`)**: `min(round((total_opened / total_delivered) * 100, 1), 100.0)`.
* **Overall RSVP Conversion (`overall_rsvp_conversion`)**: `min(round((total_attributed_rsvp / total_delivered) * 100, 1), 100.0)`.

---

## 3. Strict Metric Separation Principle

> **Core Invariant**: Broadcast-attributed metrics are strictly isolated from overall event counters.
>
> - `Event.views_count` measures ALL deduplicated views across all channels.
> - `Event.broadcast_opens_count` measures ONLY views attributed to specific broadcast recipients.
> - `Event.attendee_count` measures ALL attendees for the event.
> - `Event.broadcast_rsvp_count` measures ONLY attendees directly converted from the broadcast.

In both API schemas and the frontend UI, these numbers are displayed side-by-side with clear visual badges rather than being conflated or added together.

## 4. Views Discovery Source Breakdown

Every `EventView` record contains a clean tracking source string. The sources breakdown groups organizer event views:
- `discovery`: **Афиша** (browsing the main discovery feed, category chips, or city filter).
- `broadcast`: **Рассылки** (incoming Telegram broadcast announcements).
- `deep_link`: **Прямая ссылка** (opening an event via direct URL, t.me link, or QR code).
- `inline`: **Поделились** (Telegram inline query or forwarded event card).
- `personal`: **Мои события** (user returning from personal schedule).
- `organizer`: **Кабинет** (organizer viewing their own event).
- `unknown`: **Другое** (fallback / untracked direct navigation).

Percentages are calculated dynamically relative to `total_views` with a zero-division guard.

## 5. Grounded Factual Summary Sentence

Instead of fabricated AI recommendations or vague projections, the system computes a grounded factual statement:
- If zero data: `"Создайте первое событие или организацию, чтобы начать привлекать аудиторию."`
- If broadcast conversions exist: `"Рассылки принесли {N} переходов и {M} подтвержденных гостей на ваши события"`
- If broadcast opens exist: `"Рассылки принесли {N} переходов на страницы ваших событий"`
- If recent subscriber growth: `"+{K} новых подписчиков за последние 7 дней"`
- If attendees exist: `"{N} гостей подтвердили участие в ваших событиях"`
- If views exist: `"Ваши события просмотрели {N} раз"`
- Fallback: `"События опубликованы и готовы к привлечению гостей"`

---

## 6. Privacy & Security Safeguards

1. **Strict Ownership Scoping**: Organizers can only inspect metrics for events and organizations they own. Cross-tenant queries are blocked with `HTTP 403 Forbidden` / filtered in SQL.
2. **Aggregated Only**: All endpoints return aggregate counts and conversion rates. No `telegram_id`, phone number, recipient username, or private profile information is exposed.
3. **Zero Schema Migrations**: All metrics leverage existing indexes on `event_views`, `subscriptions`, `events`, `event_attendees`, `event_interests`, `broadcasts`, and `broadcast_recipients`.
