## 1. Executive Summary

This investigation provides a definitive answer to the legal and technical feasibility of accepting paid subscriptions for **Ivently Pro** under the Russian self-employed tax regime (**Налог на профессиональный доход / НПД**, Федеральный закон № 422-ФЗ), focusing on acquiring integration, receipt generation, and operational scalability.

### Key Conclusions:
1. **SaaS Under NPD is Legally Permissible:** Providing access to Ivently Pro (direct Telegram marketing broadcasts, audience analytics, attendee management) is classified as an information service / digital software subscription developed by the creator, fully permissible under 422-ФЗ (tax rates: 4% from individuals, 6% from legal entities/IPs).
2. **Receipt for Every Transaction is Mandatory:** Federal Law № 422-ФЗ (art. 14) mandates the generation of a fiscal receipt in «Мой налог» **at the moment of calculation** for every electronic payment. Failure to issue a receipt incurs a fine of 20% of the transaction amount (100% on repeat violation within 6 months, art. 129.13 Tax Code RF).
3. **Official "Мой налог" Public Developer API Does NOT Exist:** The Federal Tax Service (ФНС) does not provide a public REST API for individual self-employed developers. Direct programmatic access to «Мой налог» is granted exclusively to accredited credit institutions (banks) and registered electronic platform operators meeting stringent FNS security audits. Unofficial scrapers or reverse-engineered mobile APIs are strictly rejected due to critical credential and security risks.
4. **YooKassa Discontinued Self-Employed Receipt Services:** As officially documented in YooKassa's developer changelog and receipt documentation, **YooKassa terminated support for automated self-employed receipts as of December 29, 2025**. While YooKassa continues to process online acquiring payments (cards, SBP, SberPay, T-Pay) for self-employed accounts, **it no longer registers income or generates receipts in «Мой налог»**.
5. **Recommended MVP Strategy:** Launch D6.1 with **YooKassa Hosted Checkout** (0 ₽ setup, 0 ₽ monthly maintenance) paired with a **semi-manual assisted receipt workflow**. The backend will record payment orders, notify the project owner via Telegram upon successful payment, and enable pasting the «Мой налог» receipt URL for the customer. This model is operationally viable up to 15–20 payments/day. If transaction volume surpasses this threshold, Ivently will transition to an authorized partner platform (e.g. Robokassa/Prodamus) or register as an **IP on NPD** with automated banking infrastructure.

## 3. Part 1 — Self-Employed NPD Legal Framework

### 3.1 Can a Self-Employed Individual Sell Ivently Pro (SaaS)?
**YES.** Under Article 6, Clause 1 of 422-ФЗ, taxable income includes proceeds from the sale of goods, works, services, and property rights.
- Ivently is an original software product developed by the author. Providing access to Pro tools (broadcast dispatches, analytics, attendee filters) represents an information service / granting of non-exclusive digital access rights.
- It does **not** fall under the prohibitions of Article 4, Clause 2 (no resale of third-party goods, no agency/commission activities on behalf of third parties, no employees under employment contracts).
- Ministry of Finance guidance explicitly confirms that developers selling access to their own web applications, bots, and digital tools are fully entitled to apply NPD.

### 3.2 Tax Rates
Under Article 10 of 422-ФЗ:
- **4%**: For payments received from **private individuals** (физические лица).
- **6%**: For payments received from **individual entrepreneurs (ИП)** or **legal entities (ООО/АО)** for use in their commercial operations.

*Practical SaaS Application:*
When an event organizer purchases Pro on Ivently using a personal bank card or personal SBP without requesting an invoice with company requisites, the transaction is processed as a purchase by a private individual (**4%** tax). If an organizer requests a receipt issued to their registered IP or LLC (providing an INN), the transaction is registered in «Мой налог» with that INN (**6%** tax).

### 3.3 Annual Income Limit & Overrun
- **Limit:** **2,400,000 RUB per calendar year** (approx. 200,000 RUB/month).
- **If Exceeded:** The taxpayer loses the right to apply NPD on the date the threshold is breached. Income earned beyond 2.4M RUB becomes subject to personal income tax (НДФЛ 13–15%) unless the individual registers as an IP within 20 calendar days and transitions to the Simplified Tax System (УСН 6% «Доходы»).

### 3.4 Payment Gateways, Public Offer & Consumer Terms
- Accepting payments via online acquiring (ЮKassa, SBP, cards) does not alter NPD tax status. The gross amount paid by the customer is recognized as income (before acquiring fee deduction).
- Selling digital subscriptions via Telegram Mini App or web requires a **Public Offer (Публичная оферта)** and **Privacy Policy (Политика конфиденциальности)** accessible to users before payment, disclosing seller identity (Full Name, INN), service terms, price, and cancellation rules (Law of the RF «On Protection of Consumer Rights»).

## 4. Part 2 — Receipt Obligations & Sanctions

### 4.1 Is a Receipt Required for Every Payment?
**YES, unconditionally.** Under Article 14, Clause 1 of 422-ФЗ, the taxpayer is obligated to generate a receipt in «Мой налог» upon receiving income and transmit it to the purchaser.

### 4.2 Generation Timing
- For transactions settled via **electronic payment methods (bank cards, SBP, online acquiring)**, the receipt must be generated **at the moment of settlement** (п. 3 ст. 14 422-ФЗ).
- For non-electronic bank transfers directly to a settlement account, the deadline is no later than the 9th day of the following calendar month.
- *Online acquiring qualifies as an electronic payment method*, meaning receipts must be issued promptly upon payment confirmation.

### 4.3 Mandatory Receipt Requisites (п. 6 ст. 14 422-ФЗ)
- Document title («Чек»).
- Date and exact time of calculation.
- Full name and INN of the seller.
- Designation of special tax regime («Налог на профессиональный доход»).
- Description of service (e.g. «Подписка Ivently Pro на 1 месяц»).
- Total amount in RUB.
- INN of the purchaser (mandatory only if the customer is an IP or legal entity).
- Unique QR-code and fiscal receipt number.

### 4.4 Delivery & Electronic Format
- Receipts can be delivered electronically: via link (e.g. `https://lknpd.nalog.ru/api/v1/receipt/...`), email, SMS, or QR code display in the app.

### 4.5 Sanctions for Non-Compliance (ст. 129.13 НК РФ)
- Failure to generate or transmit a receipt incurs a fine of **20% of the settlement amount**.
- Repeated failure within 6 months incurs a fine of **100% of the settlement amount**.

### 4.6 Refunds & Cancellations
- When a payment is refunded, the seller must open «Мой налог», find the corresponding receipt, and select **«Аннулировать чек»** with the reason *«Возврат средств»*. The accrued tax is recalculated and cancelled automatically by the tax authority.

## 5. Part 3 — Can "Мой налог" Be Automated?

| Vector | Availability | Official Assessment |
|---|:---:|---|
| **A. Public REST API for Self-Employed** | **NO** | The FNS does not provide personal API keys or public endpoints for individual accounts. |
| **B. Closed FNS Platform Integration Protocol** | **YES** | Available exclusively to accredited banks and registered platform operators under 422-ФЗ agreements. |
| **C. YooKassa Automated Receipts** | **DISCONTINUED** | Officially discontinued by YooKassa as of **December 29, 2025**. |
| **D. Alternative Provider Automation** | **LIMITED** | Available only via specialized platforms (e.g. Robokassa «Робочеки для самозанятых», Prodamus). |
| **E. Reverse-Engineered / Scraper Scripts** | **PROHIBITED** | Insecure, brittle, violates terms of service, and risks account compromise. |

### Technical Analysis:
1. **FNS Integration Boundary:** The official FNS interaction protocol operates through a dedicated secure gateway designed for large aggregator platforms (such as Yandex Go, Avito, YouDo) and banks. Becoming an authorized platform requires legal entity status, bilateral agreements with FNS, and cryptographic information security certifications. An early-stage startup operating under individual self-employment cannot register as an FNS platform operator.
2. **YooKassa's Formal Position:** YooKassa's developer documentation explicitly confirms that self-employed income registration must be done manually by the taxpayer; YooKassa no longer offers automated receipt dispatch for self-employed merchants.

## 8. Part 6 — Operational Workload Analysis (Manual Receipts)

If Ivently operates with assisted manual receipt creation under YooKassa, the operational burden evaluates as follows:

| Daily Volume | Monthly Volume | Daily Time Burden (Estimated) | Operational Feasibility | Risk of Non-Compliance |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **1–5 payments/day** | 30–150/mo | **2–5 minutes/day** | **Completely Feasible** | Very Low (handled in single daily batch) |
| **10–20 payments/day** | 300–600/mo | **15–25 minutes/day** | **Manageable** for 1 person | Moderate (requires daily discipline) |
| **50 payments/day** | ~1,500/mo | **1–1.5 hours/day** | **Unsuitable** (high friction) | High (fatigue, delayed receipts, fine risks) |
| **100+ payments/day** | ~3,000/mo | **2–3+ hours/day** | **Critical Failure** | Severe |

### Workload Conclusion:
For the initial validation phase (**0–100 paying organizers**, generating ~3–5 payments per day), manual receipt issuance takes less than 5 minutes daily. It is economically and operationally sound to accept manual receipt generation during early MVP rather than paying 10,000 ₽ setup fees or 5–7% acquiring commissions to third parties.

## 12. Part 10 — Recommended Compliant MVP Model

```
Merchant: Self-Employed Individual (НПД)
Payment Gateway: YooKassa (Hosted Checkout: Cards + SBP)
Price Hypothesis: Dynamic server setting (e.g. PRO_MONTHLY_PRICE_RUB)
Billing Model: One-time 30-day access (Manual Renewal)
Receipt Architecture: Assisted Semi-Manual Workflow
```

### Operational Workflow:
1. Customer purchases Pro via YooKassa Hosted Checkout.
2. YooKassa dispatches verified webhook `payment.succeeded`.
3. Ivently backend instantly activates Pro for 30 days (`OrganizationPlan.expires_at`).
4. Backend creates an audit entry in `payment_receipt_logs` with status `PENDING_MANUAL_RECEIPT`.
5. Backend sends an automated administrative notification via Telegram bot to the project owner:
   > 🔔 **Новая оплата Ivently Pro!**  
   > Заказ: `#8fa19491`  
   > Организация: `Концертный Зал Махачкала`  
   > Сумма: `499 ₽` (Плательщик: физлицо, налог 4% = 19.96 ₽)  
   > ➡️ *Сформируйте чек в «Мой налог» и вставьте ссылку.*
6. The owner opens «Мой налог» on their phone (takes ~30 seconds), registers income, and pastes the receipt link (`https://lknpd.nalog.ru/api/v1/receipt/...`) via admin command / workspace.
7. The customer receives the receipt link in their organizer dashboard under billing history.

## 13. Part 11 — Direct Answers to the 14 Primary Questions

1. **Can Ivently legally sell Pro under NPD?**  
   **YES.** Selling digital software subscriptions / information access created by the author is fully compliant with 422-ФЗ.
2. **Is the tax 4% or 6% for our expected customers?**  
   **4%** for standard card/SBP payments from individuals; **6%** if an IP or LLC INN is submitted.
3. **Is a receipt required for every payment?**  
   **YES.** Mandatory upon settlement; failure carries a 20% penalty (100% on repeat violation).
4. **Can "Мой налог" receipts currently be created automatically through an official API?**  
   **NO.** There is no open public developer API for self-employed individuals.
5. **Can YooKassa automatically create the NPD receipt?**  
   **NO.** Officially discontinued as of December 29, 2025.
6. **If not, is there an official intermediary that can?**  
   **YES**, platforms like Robokassa and Prodamus, but they introduce higher commission fees (5–7%) or 10,000 ₽ setup barriers.
7. **If not, exactly what manual action is required?**  
   Registering the sale in the «Мой налог» app upon payment confirmation (takes ~30–45 seconds per payment).
8. **How does this work with SBP?**  
   YooKassa generates an SBP QR/deep link; upon customer payment, the standard webhook fires; manual receipt is issued identically.
9. **How does this work with cards?**  
   Customer inputs card details on YooKassa checkout; webhook fires; receipt is issued identically.
10. **What happens on refunds?**  
    Merchant triggers refund in YooKassa (`POST /v3/refunds`) and annuls the receipt in «Мой налог» with reason «Возврат средств».
11. **Is YooKassa still the best MVP provider?**  
    **YES.** Modern REST API, 0 ₽ fixed cost, trusted brand, cards + SBP, lowest commission for MVP stage.
12. **Should we start as self-employed or IP?**  
    **Start as self-employed.** Zero administrative friction, zero mandatory contributions, instant start. Transition to IP when volume reaches ~20+ payments/day.
13. **At what payment volume does manual receipts become unreasonable?**  
    Above **20–30 payments per day** (over 30 minutes of daily manual effort).
14. **What is the simplest legally compliant MVP architecture?**  
    YooKassa Hosted Checkout + instant Pro activation on webhook + assisted Telegram notification for manual receipt registration.
